# EXP-0082: multithreaded candidate encoding

## Hypothesis

`cmax` encodes every candidate representation one after another, so its
wall time is the sum of independent work. On text, the wide match index is
19–38% of encode time and the wide parse-and-code chain 55–67% (CPU profile,
book1 and world192.txt). Three parts are independent of each other's
results:

1. Each position's match search, once the hash chains exist.
2. Each field set's entropy-coding candidates (modes 9–13) and the final
   hybrid encodings (modes 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24).
3. A block's legacy, delta, wide, small-alphabet and BWT candidates.

Running them on worker threads should cut `cmax` wall time by at least 2x on
an idle 8-core M1 without changing a single output byte.

## Design

- `BITE_THREADS` sets the worker count. Unset or invalid means 1, which runs
  every task in its original order on the calling thread, so default
  behaviour and timings are unchanged.
- Workers only fill their own result slots. Callers then compare results in
  the original order, so ties resolve exactly as before and output does not
  depend on scheduling.
- The match index is built in two passes: a sequential pass links the hash
  chains, then 64 KiB position ranges are searched in parallel. The candidate
  each search starts from equals the hash head the old single pass saw.

## Protocol

- Identity: `cmax` and `cfast` streams at 8 threads must be byte-identical to
  `main` on every development and research file, and must round-trip.
- Timing on m1-2 (idle), process harness, research corpus, 3 repeats, at 4
  and 8 threads, against the m1-2 baseline in `results/m1-2-timing-research/`.
  CPU time is unchanged or slightly higher; only wall time falls. Reference
  codecs run single-threaded, so comparisons with them must say so.

## Results

Same-run A/B on m1-2 (`scripts/ab_timing.py`, research corpus, 3 repeats,
old binary `1b288da` against `main` after the merge; every stream identical):

| Preset | Variant | Median wall | Median CPU | Speed-up |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `cmax` | old | 174.67 s | 174.63 s | 1.00x |
| `cmax` | new, 1 thread | 174.29 s | 174.25 s | 1.00x |
| `cmax` | new, 8 threads | 73.22 s | 231.27 s | 2.39x |
| `cfast` | old | 35.55 s | 35.52 s | 1.00x |
| `cfast` | new, 1 thread | 35.17 s | 35.14 s | 1.01x |
| `cfast` | new, 8 threads | 12.67 s | 50.05 s | 2.80x |

At 8 threads `cmax` wall time falls 2.39x for 32% more total CPU time
(`cfast`: 2.80x for 41% more). With one thread, the default, nothing changes.
Reference codecs run single-threaded, so these wall times must not be set
against theirs.

## Decision

KEEP. The 2x wall-time target is met for `cmax`, and output is byte-identical
at every thread count. Results: `results/m1-2-ab-exp-0082-{cmax,cfast}/`.
